# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Gonzalo Oviedo’s GONOVI personal brand and YouTube ecosystem. The authenticated connections surface serves account holders reviewing automated trades and their actual execution results on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

GONOVI is the public hub. AlgoTrend and the gold indicators are individual products. Connections let each holder manage their own broker links and understand the performance of each strategy independently.

## Brand Personality

Clear, personal, precise. Public language uses GONOVI, never the historical repository name.

## Anti-references

Avoid presenting a single indicator or BTC demo as the parent brand. Avoid decorative financial dashboards that obscure dates, execution costs or account ownership.

## Design Principles

- Preserve account and connection boundaries.
- Make performance definitions visible and consistent.
- Separate realized results from current open-position valuation.
- Keep existing theme and privacy controls coherent across devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labelled native controls, keyboard navigation and text equivalents for charts. Distinguish positive and negative values using signs as well as color.
